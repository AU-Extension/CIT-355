KPI explorer

A key performance indicator (KPI) is a number a manager watches to know whether things are going well. Explore the on-field and business KPIs of the Biscayne Bay Sports Group, change the inputs, and see how each one is calculated.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose a team. Every calculator starts with that team's real numbers from last season.
  2. Read what each KPI means and why a manager cares about it.
  3. Change the numbers in a calculator to see how the KPI responds, for example "what if 1,000 more fans came to every game?"
  4. Use the gauge to judge the result against the course benchmark, then press "Reset to season numbers".

Benchmarks are simple course guides for this exercise, not official league standards. Season numbers come from the course data files: soccer_matches.csv, basketball_games.csv, ticket_sales.csv, and fans.csv.
