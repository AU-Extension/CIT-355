Dirty data challenge

Real data is messy. This is the raw season file exactly as it came out of the old system, with errors hidden in it. Find each problem, name it, and choose the right fix before anyone builds a report on bad numbers.

How to use this challenge
  1. Choose the soccer or basketball file. Each file has its own hidden errors and its own score.
  2. Click any cell that looks wrong. In a spreadsheet, numbers line up on the right and text lines up on the left, which is a useful clue. Away games have no attendance or ticket price on purpose: the club only records crowds at home.
  3. In the panel, choose what kind of problem it is, then choose the best fix, and press "Log this problem".
  4. Logging a cell that is actually fine costs 2 points. Press "I'm done" to see your score and any errors you missed.
