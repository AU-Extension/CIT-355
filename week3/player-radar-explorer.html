Player comparison radar

Compare scouting targets for Biscayne Bay FC or the Breakers on one chart. Every spoke shows how a player ranks against the other targets at his position, so you can see strengths and weaknesses at a glance.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose soccer or basketball, then a position.
  2. Pick two or three scouting targets from the list.
  3. Read the radar and the plain-language summary on the right.
  4. Check the table to see the real numbers behind each percentile.
  5. Scroll down to see why rates (per 90 or per 36) compare players more fairly than totals.
Pick up to 3 scouting targets to compare. Click a selected player again to remove them.

Percentile radar

Each spoke is a percentile: 100 means best among the scouting targets at this position, 50 means middle of the group. For injuries and turnovers, fewer is better, so a high score means few.
