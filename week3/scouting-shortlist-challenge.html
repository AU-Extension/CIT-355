Scouting shortlist challenge

You are the head of scouting. In each round the coach and the general manager give you a need and a budget. Shortlist two players who fit, then pick your top choice.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read the need and the must-haves for each round, including the tie-breaker.
  2. Click two player cards to shortlist them.
  3. Choose your top choice and submit.
  4. Each shortlisted player who meets every must-have earns 8 points; the best possible top choice earns 9.
  5. Play all four rounds (two soccer, two basketball) for a score out of 100.
