Data storytelling: choose the right chart

A board member gives your chart about five seconds. Pick the message first, then the chart that shows it best. Compare good and poor choices drawn with real Biscayne Bay data, and learn to fix charts that mislead.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose a team at the top. The charts redraw with that team's data.
  2. Pick a message type: change over time, comparison, part of a whole, relationship, or ranking.
  3. Compare the good chart with the poor chart and read why one works better.
  4. Scroll to "Fix this chart" and use the buttons to see each misleading chart corrected.
  5. Finish with the five quick questions.
