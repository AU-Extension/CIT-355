Board meeting challenge

You are presenting five slides to the Biscayne Bay Sports Group board. For each slide, pick the headline that tells the story best, then answer the board member's tough question. The numbers come from the course data.

How to use this challenge
  1. Study the chart and the note under it.
  2. Step 1: choose the best headline for the slide (8 points).
  3. Step 2: read the board member's question and choose the strongest answer (12 points).
  4. Press "Check my answers" to see why, then go to the next slide. Five slides, 100 points.

What strong presenters do

  • Write headlines that state the insight and why it matters, not just the topic.
  • Say how much evidence sits behind a number (one game or six games?).
  • Separate "goes together" from "causes," and propose a test when it matters.
  • Never overpromise. A clear "it lowers the risk" beats a false "it guarantees."
