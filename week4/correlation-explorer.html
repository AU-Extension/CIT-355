What is a correlation?

A correlation is a number from -1 to +1 that shows how closely two things move together. Close to +1: when one goes up, the other usually goes up. Close to -1: when one goes up, the other usually goes down. Close to 0: no clear pattern. A correlation never proves that one thing causes the other. A third factor can push both at the same time, and managers who miss it make expensive decisions.
