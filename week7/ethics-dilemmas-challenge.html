Ethics dilemmas challenge

Ten real-world style situations from the Biscayne Bay Sports Group front office. For each one, choose the most responsible action and name the principle at stake: privacy, consent, fairness, transparency, or accountability.

How to use this challenge
  1. Read the situation carefully. Each one involves data or AI.
  2. Choose the most responsible action (up to 6 points; a partly responsible action earns 2).
  3. Choose the main principle at stake (4 points).
  4. Press "Check my answer" to see the explanation, then move to the next situation.
  5. Your final score and a summary by principle appear at the end. Press "Start over" to try again.
