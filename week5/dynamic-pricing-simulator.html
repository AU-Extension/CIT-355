Dynamic pricing simulator

Set ticket prices for one home game and watch what happens to tickets sold, revenue, and how fans feel. Change the opponent, the day, the weather, and the promotion to see why the same seat is worth more on some nights than others.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose Biscayne Bay FC (soccer) or the Biscayne Bay Breakers (basketball).
  2. Describe the game: opponent strength, rivalry, day, weather (soccer only), and promotion.
  3. Move the price slider for each seating section. Every result updates right away.
  4. Pick a section under "Revenue curve" to see where its ticket revenue peaks.
  5. Watch the fan sentiment meter: the highest revenue is not always the smartest price.

All numbers come from a simple model built for the fictional Biscayne Bay clubs, based on the patterns in the course file ticket_sales.csv. Concessions use $23 per fan, the average spent per game in fans.csv.
