Season revenue challenge

You are the ticketing manager for a six-game home stretch. Set a price level and a promotion for each game to reach the revenue target while keeping the venue full and the fans on your side.

How to use this challenge
  1. Pick your team. Each team has its own six games and its own target.
  2. Read each game card: opponent, day, rivalry, and weather tell you how strong demand will be.
  3. Set the price level (100% means standard prices in every section) and choose a promotion.
  4. The card shows the forecast attendance and fan mood. Revenue stays hidden until you submit, just like a real season.
  5. Submit your plan to see revenue, your score out of 100, and feedback on every game. You can try again.

Game-day revenue means ticket revenue plus concessions ($23 per fan, the average in fans.csv) minus the cost of the promotion. The demand model is the same one used in the dynamic pricing simulator.
