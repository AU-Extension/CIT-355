Analytics ladder

Sports analytics answers four kinds of questions, from "what happened?" to "what should we do?". Climb the ladder with real numbers from the Biscayne Bay Sports Group and see how each step gets you closer to a decision.

How to use this interactive
  1. Pick a team with the Soccer or Basketball switch.
  2. Click each rung of the ladder, starting at the bottom, and read the example for that level.
  3. On the predictive rung, change the game facts and watch the attendance forecast move.
  4. On the prescriptive rung, read the recommendation and the "check" questions a manager should ask.
  5. Finish with the sorting game: place eight front office questions on the right rung.

The four levels

Each level builds on the one below it. Higher levels are worth more to a decision, and they are harder to get right.

Sorting game: which rung is this question on?

Read each question a front office might ask and choose its level. You get instant feedback.
