Gut or data?

Ten real decisions from the Biscayne Bay Sports Group front office. Make a quick gut call, look at the data, then make your final decision. You score points for the decision the data supports, and you will see when experience still matters.

How to use this challenge
  1. Read the situation and make your gut call. It does not count toward your score.
  2. Read the data card that appears. All numbers come from last season.
  3. Make your final decision. It is worth 10 points if it matches what the data supports.
  4. Read the explanation and the note on what data cannot see, then go to the next decision.
